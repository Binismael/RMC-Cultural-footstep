Reconnecting Youth with Yoruba Heritage

RMC Cultural Footprints is a movement designed to revive and promote Yoruba culture through educational tourism, storytelling, and experiential learning. Each tour becomes a living classroom where students explore history, tradition, and identity firsthand.

Our Mission & Objectives

🏛

Promote Yoruba Heritage

Empowering youth to explore, appreciate, and preserve Yoruba traditions and cultural values.

📚

Bridge Learning with Experience

Transforming classroom lessons into hands-on cultural exploration and real-world discovery.

🤝

Support Local Communities

Engaging artisans, historians, and custodians to sustain cultural tourism and heritage.

Explore, Learn, and Celebrate Yoruba Culture

🏛

Historical Tours

Guided visits to Ilé-Ifẹ̀, Ọ̀yọ́, Ọ̀ṣun-Ọ̀ṣogbo Grove and more historical sites.

🎭

Cultural Performances

Traditional music, dance, and storytelling showcasing Yoruba artistic heritage.

📖

Language Workshops

Proverbs, riddles, and Yoruba poetry challenges to master our native language.

🎮

Cultural Games

Ayo Olopon, debates, and team tasks celebrating traditional Yoruba games.

📷

Media Coverage

Documenting each journey for education, archives, and future inspiration.
